A growing collection of references, tutorials, lectures, and practical tools for learning radio astronomy and 21 cm cosmology.

I am building this page as a curated starting point for students and researchers entering radio astronomy and 21 cm cosmology. I will add resources gradually, prioritizing material that I have found especially clear or useful in practice.
