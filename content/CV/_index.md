## Honors and awards

- Trottier Chair Fellowship, 2021-2026

- DST INSPIRE Fellowship for doctoral research, 2016-2021
- Scholarship for Higher Education, DST-INSPIRE, 2010-2015

## Collaboration roles

- **CHIME:** science lead for the first cosmological 21 cm auto-power spectrum detection; member of the HI intensity-mapping and pipeline groups.
- **CHORD:** member of the calibration and intensity-mapping group and the HI galaxy survey group.
- **SKAO:** member of the Epoch of Reionization and Cosmic Dawn group and the Cosmology group; lead of the SKA-India “Akashganga” foreground-challenge team.

## Community service

Referee for MNRAS, Astronomy & Astrophysics, the Open Journal of Astrophysics, and Publications of the Astronomical Society of Australia; referee for the GMRT Time Allocation Committee.
