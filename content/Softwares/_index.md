My research is built around reproducible, end-to-end analysis. I contribute to collaboration software and maintain tools developed for my own observing programs.

<div class="card-grid">
  <article class="info-card">
    <p class="card-meta">Python · radiocosmology</p>
    <h2><a href="https://github.com/radiocosmology/draco">draco</a></h2>
    <p>Pipeline building blocks for the analysis and simulation of data from transit radio interferometers. I contribute analysis components used in CHIME and related 21 cm workflows.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="info-card">
    <p class="card-meta">Python · CHIME</p>
    <h2><a href="https://github.com/chime-experiment/ch_pipeline">ch_pipeline</a></h2>
    <p>The CHIME analysis pipeline, including data-processing infrastructure for calibration, beamforming, foreground filtering, and validation.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="info-card">
    <p class="card-meta">Python · telescope modelling</p>
    <h2><a href="https://github.com/radiocosmology/driftscan">driftscan</a></h2>
    <p>Models transfer functions for drift-scan telescopes and generates products used to filter foregrounds and estimate 21 cm power spectra.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="info-card">
    <p class="card-meta">Python · sky simulation</p>
    <h2><a href="https://github.com/radiocosmology/cora">cora</a></h2>
    <p>Simulates low-frequency radio skies, including polarized Galactic synchrotron emission, point sources, and cosmological 21 cm signals.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="info-card">
    <p class="card-meta">CASA · uGMRT</p>
    <h2><a href="https://github.com/Arnab-half-blood-prince/GMRT_DI_Calibration_pipeline">GMRT DI Calibration Pipeline</a></h2>
    <p>My public direction-independent calibration and RFI-flagging pipeline for uGMRT Band 3, Band 4, and Band 5 data.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="info-card">
    <p class="card-meta">Validation · CHIME</p>
    <h2><a href="https://github.com/chime-experiment/Daily_validation">Daily validation</a></h2>
    <p>Notebooks and diagnostics used to monitor analysis products and test the stability of CHIME data-processing outputs.</p>
  </article>
</div>

More repositories and contributions are available on [GitHub](https://github.com/Arnab-half-blood-prince).
